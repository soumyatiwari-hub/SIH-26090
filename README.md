# AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application

## Problem Statement
AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Mobile Application for Marginalized Artisans.

## Overview
This project is designed to help marginalized artisans and weavers showcase, catalog, and sell their products through a digital platform.

The application uses Artificial Intelligence to simplify product cataloging and help artisans connect with potential customers and markets.

## Objectives
- Provide artisans with a digital marketplace.
- Help create product catalogs easily.
- Use AI for smart product categorization.
- Improve market visibility for artisans.
- Connect artisans directly with customers.
- Support better income opportunities.

## Key Features
- Artisan registration and profiles
- Product listing
- AI-based product categorization
- Smart catalog generation
- Search and filtering
- Digital marketplace
- Customer interaction
- Product images and descriptions
- Sales and market insights
